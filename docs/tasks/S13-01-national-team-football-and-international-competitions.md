# S13-01 — Implement national team football, international call-ups, and tournaments

**Status:** Not started  
**Sprint:** 13 — International world and careers  
**Source:** PRD §34; technical plan §16; OPENCODE.md  
**Depends on:** S04-01, S08-02

## What to do

Build national team management and international tournament processing. Top-performing managers can be appointed as national team managers alongside their club role (or as dedicated jobs). Implement national squad selection windows, international match calendars, continental/world cup tournaments, and international reputation impact on player market valuation.

## Acceptance criteria

- National team rosters call up eligible players dynamically based on nationality, active form, and reputation.
- International tournament fixtures run during designated calendar windows without domestic fixture collisions.
- Performing well in international tournaments boosts player international prestige, market value, and wage demands.
- National team managers select call-up squads, manage tactics, and compete in major international trophies.
- International caps and goals are recorded permanently in player history read-models.
