# S16-01 — Implement global macro-economic simulation and TV rights distribution

**Status:** Not started  
**Sprint:** 16 — Global economy and fair monetization  
**Source:** PRD §77; technical plan §16; OPENCODE.md  
**Depends on:** S05-02, S14-02

## What to do

Build the macro-economic engine governing world financial inflation/deflation, cross-league TV broadcast rights distribution, financial fair play (FFP) regulations, and economic tier balancing across international leagues.

## Acceptance criteria

- Macro-economic engine adjusts annual TV rights pool allocations based on world viewership trends and league reputation.
- Financial Fair Play rules monitor squad wage-to-turnover ratios, penalizing excessive debt accumulation via transfer bans or points deductions.
- Transfer market price inflation/deflation scales dynamically with total liquid capital in the world economy.
- All macro-economic transfers and TV revenue distributions append cleanly to `finance.ledger_entries`.
- World financial health dashboards display league revenue rankings and economic indicators.
